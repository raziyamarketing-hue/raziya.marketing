Short-form
Video Concepts

Video 1 — Sample Script

Shot IdeaWalk into a bright 3-bedroom townhouse garden in a suburban community like Town Square or Damac Hills 2.
On-Screen Text (0:00)3-bed villa in dubai = how much?? 🏡
Hook (0:00–0:03)“Everyone thinks renting a villa in Dubai costs half a million dirhams. Guess the rent on this 3-bedroom.”
Fast Cuts (0:03–0:14)“Private backyard. Covered parking for two cars. Pool and park right outside the gate. Drop your number—3, 2, 1...”
The Reveal & Lesson (0:14–0:24)“AED 135,000 a year. That’s less than a cramped 2-bedroom in Downtown—and why families are flooding into outer Dubai communities right now.”
CTA (0:24–0:30)“Looking for a family townhouse to rent or buy without overpaying? Message me ‘VILLA’ and I’ll send today’s best options.”

Video 2 — Sample Script

Shot IdeaWalk backwards through a massive, bright 1-bedroom apartment in JVC or Dubai Hills, pointing at the big balcony and open kitchen.
On-Screen Text (0:00)guess the yearly rent in 5 secs 👀
Hook (0:00–0:03)“You have 5 seconds to guess the yearly rent on this massive Dubai flat.”
Fast Tour Cuts (0:03–0:14)“900 square feet. Huge balcony. Brand-new gym downstairs. And it’s 15 minutes from Marina. Drop your guess in the comments—3, 2, 1...”
The Reveal & Investor Lesson (0:14–0:24)“It’s AED 72,000 a year. And the owner bought it off-plan 2 years ago for AED 780k—making a clean 9.2% return today.”
CTA (0:24–0:30)“Want to see which off-plan launches right now can pull these numbers? Comment ‘RENT’ and I’ll DM you the top 3.”
